Declutter - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android game "Declutter" (the "App"), published by Naazim ("I", "me"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress (levels completed, house restoration progress, daily drawer state, settings such as sound and music) locally on your device. This data is not sent to me. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up this save file to your Google account (Google Drive) so it can be restored when you reinstall the App or set up a new phone; this backup is handled by Google and you can manage it in your device's backup settings. Otherwise the data is removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Information I collect

I do not collect, store, or receive any personal information directly. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server. Analytics and advertising data is collected by Google through the services described below.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • App interaction and ad performance data (ad views, clicks, crashes and diagnostics)

This data is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ads. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

I use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are completed and how long sessions last). It collects an app-instance identifier, device information and in-game events. This data is processed by Google; see the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. I use this data only to improve the game. Analytics only starts after the consent step described below; where consent is required, it follows the choices you make there.

5. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the App saves a copy of your game progress (levels, stars, house progress, daily streak and hints) to your Google Play Games account using Saved Games, so it is kept when you reinstall the App or change phones. This copy is stored and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the App uses your Play Games player identity only to read and write it. You can delete saved game data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

6. In-app purchase (Google Play)

The App offers one optional purchase, "Remove Ads". Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; I never see your payment details. The App asks Google Play whether you own the purchase so it can restore it after a reinstall. Google Play's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

7. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK and other regions): where required by law, the App shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later where the App offers a privacy options setting.
  • Reset or limit ad personalization and analytics identifiers: on your Android device, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact me and I will help resolve it.

9. Data sharing and security

I do not sell or share personal information. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play and Google Play Games), as described above. Because I do not collect personal data, I hold none to protect, but I encourage you to keep your device up to date.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights over data that Google collects through ads and analytics. Since I hold no personal data, you can exercise these rights through your Google account privacy controls at myaccount.google.com or by contacting me below.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
